# Product Vision & Visual Personality 🌿

## The Core Sensation

> *"Opening a tiny peaceful reading room that happens to have a garden growing around it."*

**Nook** is created to be an antidote to digital noise. Where modern apps use urgency, notifications, loud gradients, and gamified pressure to demand attention, Nook offers stillness, warmth, and deliberate focus.

When a reader opens Nook, the atmosphere should instantly feel like stepping into a sunlit library nook surrounded by trailing vines and potted ferns, with a warm cup of tea resting on the windowsill.

---

## The Visual Direction & Palette

The design aesthetic of Nook is **cozy, restrained, and elegant**. It evokes physical paper, natural daylight, and living flora without crossing into juvenile or cartoonish aesthetics.

### 🎨 Harmonious Color Palette

* **Canvas / Background:** Warm cream and soft linen (`#FAF7F2`, `#F4EFE6`) — easy on the eyes for extended reading sessions, avoiding harsh pure whites.
* **Primary Accent (Flora):** Sage green (`#7E9A82`, `#9BB09E`) — soothing, natural, and representative of living growth.
* **Secondary Accent (Petals & Highlights):** Dusty pink (`#D8A49B`, `#E8C5BE`) — soft warmth for bookmarks, highlights, and budding flowers.
* **Tertiary Accent (Atmosphere):** Lavender & Muted Lilac (`#B2A4C0`, `#D5CDE0`) — gentle dusk tones for nighttime reading modes and chapter transitions.
* **Typography & Ink:** Warm espresso brown and deep charcoal (`#2D2420`, `#423730`) — softer than jet black, providing crisp readability with natural ink contrast.
* **Borders & Dividers:** Soft sandstone and translucent taupe (`#E5DDD0`) — subtle structural boundaries that never compete for attention.

### 📐 Layout, Geometry & Texture

* **Rounded but Restrained:** Elements feature gentle, ergonomic corner radii (8px to 16px) that soften the interface without becoming bubble-like.
* **Generous Whitespace:** Spacious margins, breathable line spacing (1.6–1.8), and uncluttered layouts let the text breathe.
* **Subtle Elevation:** Soft, diffused, warm-tinted shadows that simulate natural ambient daylight rather than harsh digital drop shadows.
* **Illustrative Touch:** Hand-drawn vector botanical accents, delicate leaf chapter dividers, and gentle plant growth states.
* **Micro-Animations:** Micro-interactions that feel organic: a gentle leaf rustle when bookmarking a page, smooth fade transitions between chapters, and gradual growth animations when opening the garden.

### ⚖️ Aesthetic Principles

| Nook is... | Nook is NOT... |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cozy & Warm** | Childish or cartoonish |
| **Minimalist & Serene** | Cold, stark, or overly clinical |
| **Tactile & Organic** | Skeuomorphic or cluttered with heavy textures |
| **Focused on Timeless Classics** | A noisy storefront or social network |
| **Calm & Self-Paced** | Gamified with streaks, alerts, and badges |

## The End-to-End User Experience

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[Discover] --> B[Read]
    B --> C[Track Progress]
    C --> D[Take Notes]
    D --> E[Grow Garden]
    E --> F[Understand & Remember]
```

### 1. Discover
Readers explore a curated library of public-domain literature. Instead of algorithmic recommendations designed for clicks, books are organized by:
* Mood (e.g., *Quiet Contemplation*, *Adventurous Spirit*, *Gothic Melancholy*)
* Estimated reading duration (e.g., *A 30-Minute Stroll*, *An Afternoon Read*, *A Winter's Companion*)
* Historical era and literary movement

### 2. Read
The reading room is pure, distraction-free typography:
* High-quality public-domain typography honoring the original work.
* Adjustable serif typefaces (e.g., Georgia, Charter, Merriweather).
* Natural page progression and subtle progress indicators.

### 3. Track Progress
Progress is tracked automatically and deterministically as pages or chapters are read. No timers forcing urgency; just honest, quiet accounting of how much has been explored.

### 4. Take Notes & Journal
A quiet marginalia system allowing readers to:
* Highlight passages in dusty rose, sage, or soft gold.
* Add personal reflections and marginal notes.
* Review collected thoughts per chapter or per book in a personal Reading Journal.

### 5. Grow Your Reading Garden
Every book in your library is bound to a botanical seed. As you read, the plant grows deterministically based on reading progress:

| Reading Progress | Garden State | Visual Representation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0% – 9%** | **Seed** | A small seed nestled in fertile soil with a tiny glimmer of morning dew. |
| **10% – 29%** | **Sprout** | A delicate green shoot emerging through the earth with two tender leaves. |
| **30% – 59%** | **Growing Plant** | A flourishing stem with budding branches and foliage expanding in height. |
| **60% – 89%** | **Flowering Plant** | Vibrant blossoms beginning to unfurl in dusty pink, lavender, and gold. |
| **90% – 99%** | **Nearly Complete** | A rich, full-canopied botanical specimen on the verge of full bloom. |
| **100% (Finished)** | **Mature Tree / Plant** | A permanent, fully blossomed botanical monument permanently placed in your Sanctuary Grove. |

### 6. Understand & Remember
Once a book is completed, its mature plant remains in your Sanctuary Grove. Readers can tap any plant in their garden at any time to reopen its associated notes, favorite quotes, and reading timeline.

## Strict Scope Guardrails (Phase 1 & Beyond)

To protect the integrity and peaceful nature of Nook, the following boundaries are strictly enforced:

* ❌ **No User Uploads (Initial Version):** All books are sourced from verified public-domain repositories.
* ❌ **No Mandatory AI:** Core reading, notes, progress, and garden features are 100% deterministic and do not require AI.
* ❌ **No Social Pressures:** No leaderboards, public follower counts, reading speed competitions, or social feeds.
* ❌ **No Dark Patterns:** No intrusive notifications, paywalls, premium unlock tiers, or artificial time gates.
* ❌ **No Opaque Licensing:** Every book visibly displays its provenance, source URL, and public-domain rights status.
